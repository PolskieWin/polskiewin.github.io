Why LibreLauncher Wins

1

It Is Completely Free

Most Minecraft Android launchers charge a fee or lock features behind a subscription. LibreLauncher charges nothing. No freemium tier, no expiry, no upsell. The full experience is free on day one and will stay that way.

You Can See Every Line of Code

Closed-source launchers ask you to trust them blindly. LibreLauncher publishes its entire source code on GitHub. Any developer, security researcher, or curious user can audit exactly what the app does. No hidden network calls, no surprise data collection.

Built for Mobile Performance

LibreLauncher ships with JVM flags tuned specifically for ARM Android processors. That means lower garbage-collection pauses, faster chunk loading, and better frame rates compared to launchers ported from desktop with zero mobile optimisation.

Custom RAM allocation lets you squeeze every megabyte out of your device — without over-allocating and crashing your phone.

4

Real Mod Support Out of the Box

Forge, Fabric, OptiFine — all supported. The majority of free Android launchers either strip mod support entirely or only support vanilla Minecraft. With LibreLauncher you can install any mod just as you would on a desktop.

5

Official Microsoft Account Login

LibreLauncher uses the official OAuth flow to authenticate with your Microsoft account. Your credentials never pass through any third-party server. You get the same legitimate login as the official desktop launcher, which also means you can play on any server without getting flagged.

6

Controller and Gamepad Support

Touchscreen Minecraft is awkward. LibreLauncher adds native Bluetooth and USB gamepad support, turning your phone or tablet into a proper Minecraft console — no extra apps, no remapping hacks, just plug in and play.

F-Droid Submission — Community Verification

LibreLauncher has been submitted to F-Droid, the gold standard for verified open-source Android apps. No other Minecraft Android launcher goes through this level of independent scrutiny.

When F-Droid lists it, you can install directly from a trusted repository with no sideloading required.
